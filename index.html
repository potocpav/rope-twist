    Why Ropes Twist

    Climbing ropes sometimes twist and tangle, but the precise mechanism of how and when is not well understood in the climbing community. Which belay devices, anchor configurations, or coiling methods cause the twist? Does it depend on friction, climber weight, rappelling speed? What even is rope twist? This article explores the physics of rope twisting to answer these questions.

    What exactly is rope twist?

    Rope twist is simply the rotation of a rope around its own axis. When the number of rotations is large enough, the rope starts buckling, which eventually forms loops and generally tangles the rope.

    In most cases, rope twisting has nothing to do with the rope sheath moving relative to the rope core.
